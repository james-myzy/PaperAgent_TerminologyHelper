SkyJEPA: Learning Long-Horizon World Models for Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Control of Quadrotors

Pratyakh Rao¹, Wancong Zhang², Randall Balestriero³, Yann LeCun², Giuseppe Loianno¹
¹NYU Tandon　²FAIR at Meta　³Brown University
2. Method

The world model is trained entirely in latent space03: an encoder maps observations to embeddings, a predictor models their evolution under actions, and the physics-inspired prober08 decodes physical state. No pixel reconstruction is required, which keeps the training signal focused on dynamics rather than appearance.

Because the encoder stays frozen during control, the latent dynamics model07 can be reused across tasks. The prober is trained with a small amount of ground-truth state11 from simulation, then transferred without fine-tuning — the zero-shot sim-to-real10 setting.

At deployment, the predictor runs a receding-horizon rollout12 at 100 Hz13 on an onboard Jetson Orin14, keeping inference latency15 under 4 ms.

3. Experiments

We compare against model-based RL16 and end-to-end imitation17 baselines on aggressive trajectory tracking, reporting tracking error18, recovery rate19 and success under disturbance rejection20.
